Questions

The things everyone asks first

How much does a website actually cost?

Launch starts at $1,290 with the current sprint discount, Growth at $2,890, and Scale is scoped per project from $5,900. You get a fixed written quote before anything starts — we don't bill by the hour and we don't send surprise invoices.

How fast can you deliver?

You'll see a first design inside seven days of the kickoff call. A five-page Launch site is typically live in two to three weeks, e-commerce in three to four. Rush delivery is available for +35% when we have a slot.

What do you need from me?

A 30-minute call, whatever brand assets and photos you already have, and one person who can approve designs. If you don't have copy or photography, we can cover both as add-ons.

Do I own the site?

Yes. On final payment you own the design, the code and the domain. No lock-in, no proprietary builder you can't leave, no hostage hosting.

What happens after launch?

Every build includes 30 days of post-launch fixes. After that, Site Care at $149/month covers updates, backups, monitoring and monthly edits — optional, cancel any time.

Why is there a discount right now?

We take a fixed number of builds a month so nothing gets rushed. The sprint discount applies to projects booked while slots are open, and the price reverts once the month is full.
